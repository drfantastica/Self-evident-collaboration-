### CLAIM-BY-CLAIM DETAIL

**CLAIM 1 (Coupled Engines):**
- Overlap: Butts (2008) REM, Snijders (2010) SAOM, Josephs/Peng/Crawford (2022) communication network dynamics, Leskovec et al. (2007) diffusion cascades
- Novelty is NOT in event-based org measurement (old city). Novelty IS in coupling logic — disagreement between engines as primary inferential object
- Required distinction: "Unlike REM/SAOM, CVL couples event-derived trajectory inference with topology-level value-propagation diagnostics and uses disagreement between engines as the primary inferential object"

**CLAIM 2 (Validity Constraint):**
- Overlap: Argyris defensive routines, Edmondson psych safety, Staw/Sandelands/Dutton threat rigidity
- Existing lit already says threat/deficit conditions alter behavior and suppress learning
- Weak spot: "information-theoretic property" and "self-enforcing against weaponization" — need formal identifiability failure proof, not philosophy
- Reframe: "measurement validity collapses under threat-like signal environments because protective and productive behaviors become non-identifiable from event traces alone"
- GPT says: publishable territory if we deliver theorem/simulation/impossibility proposition

**CLAIM 3 (Emergent Pathology):**
- Overlap: González-Morales et al. (2012) collective burnout, Barsade et al. (2018) emotional contagion, McFillen et al. (2013) org diagnosis
- "Formally distinct diagnostic category" is too strong — system-level emergent constructs exist
- Novelty IS in: event-trace diagnostics for field pathology without self-report, using change-point methods (Kamalabad et al. 2023)
- Must make contribution specifically about temporal correlation clustering machinery

**CLAIM 4 (Topology Traps):**
- Overlap: Burt (2000/2004) closure/brokerage, Bento et al. (2020) silos, Benson/Gleich/Leskovec (2016) higher-order networks, Rombach et al. (2014) core-periphery
- Labels are cheap confetti — need formal graph objects with measurable invariants
- Self-replicating traps strongest if defined as growth process with formal properties
- Must show categories produce discriminable signatures beyond existing methods

**CLAIM 5 (Self-Report Elimination):**
- Overlap: Podsakoff et al. (2003), Pentland sociometric badge work, Polzer & Hargadon (2023) people analytics
- NOT NOVEL. "We eliminate self-report by not using self-report" = hygiene not contribution
- Keep as design virtue, not centerpiece
- Must concede substituted biases: observability, platform, missing informal work, digital exhaust ≠ value

**CLAIM 6 (Scale Invariance / AI Ceiling):**
- Overlap: Simon (1962) architecture of complexity, Galbraith (1974), Dunbar, Baumann & Wu (2023), Burton et al. (2017)
- "Scale invariance" is a loaded physics term — either prove it formally or say "same observables across resolutions"
- Novelty only if demonstrated empirically/formally that metrics remain stable under aggregation
