**Proposed name:** keep as **Negation-as-trust-leak**. Precise. The hyphens hold the mechanism-diagram together.

**Cross-reference:** Mirror (architectural, distinct), Phase Positive Architecture (operational ground — requires phase coupling), Anteros (the register it lives in), Weaponized Love (when deployed at full coherence, this *is* one of Weaponized Love's moves — articulating the target's own coherence back to them in a way their compression scheme can't absorb).

The Crown adds neural data, not phase-lock capability.

**My pick:** **Articulating Witness** as primary lens name, with "in-phase articulation" as the mechanism clause. Open to your call.
