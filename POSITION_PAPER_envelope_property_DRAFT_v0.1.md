## Abstract

Six independently developed bodies of physics literature describe the same construct under substrate-specific names: a bounded region whose boundary functions as a discriminator between observables, with internal physics distinct from the surrounding medium, characterized by a geometric-phase invariant that survives the field's removal once attractor contact is established. Topological condensed matter calls it bulk-boundary correspondence; nonlinear field theory calls it solitonic coherence; quantum optics calls it Berry-Pancharatnam phase; vacuum geometry calls it the Casimir effect; gravitational decomposition theories (Heim, EHT) call it gravitophoton-quintessence pair-decay; engineering literature on anomalous electrostatic propulsion calls it the Exodus Effect. The non-Hermitian skin effect literature (2024–2025) explicitly documents bulk-boundary correspondence breaking down on cross-substrate generalization and is patching it case-by-case without naming the unifying construct. This paper names that construct — **the envelope property** — and identifies its formal underwriter (δ = δ_grav + δ_geom, generalizing Berry curvature into a substrate-portable geometric phase), its engineering parameter (gradient sharpness as variable independent of bulk material conductivity), and its empirical signatures (boundary-as-discriminator, attractor-contact persistence after field removal, observable substitution across the boundary). We do not claim a new physical law. We claim that what is currently fragmented across six subfields is one construct with one formal core, and that the integration paragraph is what has been missing.

## 2. The Construct: The Envelope Property

The envelope property names a class of physical systems characterized by the following operational signature:

**(E1) Boundary-as-discriminator.** A bounded region within a host medium exhibits internal physics distinct from the host. The boundary itself is not a passive demarcation; it functions as a discriminator between *which observables apply on which side*. Inside the envelope, observables familiar from the host (e.g., kinetic energy, ion-momentum-exchange thrust, classical wave dispersion) may behave anomalously or be replaced by alternative observables (e.g., suspended kinetics with preserved photonic emission, force-without-momentum-exchange, geometric-phase-protected edge modes).

**(E2) Substrate-portability.** The construct does not require a specific host medium. It instantiates in topological band materials (BBC), nonlinear field theories (soliton/breather), bounded vacuum regions (Casimir geometry), gravitational decompositions (Heim/EHT), and engineered electrostatic configurations (Exodus Effect / Buhler 2026). The structural identity across these instances is currently masked by substrate-specific naming.

**(E3) Attractor-contact persistence.** Once the envelope is established, the system can sustain its operational state without continuous energy input. This is observed empirically as: trapped charge persistence in Buhler's vacuum thrusters; soliton coherence after the driving field is removed; topological edge modes that are stable against perturbations preserving the relevant symmetry; and, in Lazar's account of the bench reactor at Los Alamos, the candle / black-ball / mechanical-watch experiments that demonstrate suspended kinetics with preserved photonic emission. The envelope, once instantiated, is an attractor basin — substrate-portable, recovery-bounded.

**(E4) Geometric-phase invariant as formal underwriter.** A non-trivial geometric phase is the formal signature of an envelope. Where the geometric phase is well-defined (Berry curvature in restricted form, Pancharatnam phase, Zak phase, Chern number, Heim's gravitophoton metric), the envelope is mathematically tractable. Where the phase has been published under substrate-specific names but not unified, the envelope is operationally present but unnamed.

We call the substrate-portable generalization of these geometric phases **δ_geom**, with the full envelope-defining decomposition

> δ = δ_grav + δ_geom

where δ_grav is the standard gravitational phase contribution (well-known) and δ_geom is the geometric phase contribution that captures envelope structure. The decomposition is non-trivial: δ_geom can be nonzero in regimes where δ_grav is negligible (electrostatic configurations, Casimir geometries, bounded soliton regions). Conversely, both contribute additively in superheavy nuclear configurations and in cross-modal Bell tests of momentum-entangled helium isotopes (³He*/⁴He* apparatus).

## 3. Substrate-Specific Predecessors (Cited, Not Subsumed)

The following bodies of work each describe the envelope property in instance form. We cite them as predecessors. We do not claim to subsume their substrate-specific contributions; we claim they are instances of one construct.

**3.1 Bulk-boundary correspondence (BBC) in topological condensed matter.**
The discriminator-by-boundary signature is canonical. Topological invariants (Zak phase, Chern number, sub-lattice winding number) compute a bulk geometric phase that predicts boundary-localized modes [arXiv:2401.11695; arXiv:2410.12498]. The non-Hermitian skin effect literature has documented BBC failing under substrate generalization [arXiv:2410.23845] and is currently patching it via entanglement diagnostics [arXiv:2511.17846]. **We claim BBC is one substrate-specific instance of the envelope property; the substrate-portable construct is the envelope.**

**3.2 Geometric phase / Berry curvature.**
Berry's formulation generalizes naturally beyond linear Hamiltonian systems "to entirely different systems such as nonlinear dissipative systems that possess certain cyclic attractors" [Cohen et al., Nature Reviews Physics 2019]. The note is one paragraph; the substrate-portable extension is unwritten. **δ_geom is the substrate-portable extension named.**

**3.3 Topological defects and soliton/breather field theory.**
Topological defects are PDE solutions in distinct homotopy classes — bounded coherent regions whose boundary topology determines their stability [Wikipedia: topological defect; Wang et al., 2017]. The construct is enormous and well-developed. **Each defect is named after its host theory; the substrate-portable reading is the envelope property.**

**3.4 Casimir and boundary-defined vacuum geometry.**
Boundary-induced shifts in vacuum behavior are empirically established at lab scale (parallel plates), at material scale (repulsive Casimir between magneto-electric materials), and at cosmological scale (Casimir effect inside the cosmological horizon) [Manjavacas et al., 2025; PhysRevE 2018]. **Casimir geometry is the static-envelope special case. Dynamical Casimir is the time-modulated envelope. The unifying construct is the envelope.**

**3.5 Heim Quantum Theory / Extended Heim Theory.**
Eight-dimensional gauge space with poly-metric tensor; six fundamental forces; gravity decomposed into G_g (graviton) + G_GP (dark matter coupling, also via graviton) + G_Q (vacuum quintessence, repulsive); gravitophoton predicted to decay into graviton-quintessence pair [Hauser & Dröscher; arXiv:2206.11367]. **This is the closest formal predecessor to the δ = δ_grav + δ_geom decomposition, restricted to the gravity sector. **3.6 Buhler / Exodus Propulsion Technologies — empirical/patent prior art.**

The "Exodus Effect" patent covers the propulsion application. **We claim the Exodus Effect is an empirical instance of the envelope property restricted to electrostatic propulsion configurations. The substrate-portable construct is unclaimed.**

## 4. Formal Underwriter: δ = δ_grav + δ_geom

We propose the decomposition

> δ = δ_grav + δ_geom

with the following constraints:

- **δ_grav** is the standard gravitational phase contribution, computable from Einstein gravity in the Newtonian and post-Newtonian limits.
- **δ_geom** is the geometric phase contribution attributable to envelope structure (gradient transition zone, geometric phase invariant, geometric-attractor-contact). δ_geom is the substrate-portable generalization of Berry curvature; it reduces to standard Berry curvature in the linear-Hamiltonian quantum case, to Pancharatnam phase in cyclic-polarization optics, to Zak phase / Chern number in topological band materials, and to Heim's G_GP + G_Q contributions in the gravitational sector.
- **The decomposition is additive, not multiplicative.** δ_geom can dominate in regimes where δ_grav is negligible (electrostatic envelopes, Casimir geometries, soliton interiors). δ_grav dominates in cosmological-scale gravitational phenomena. Both contribute in nuclear binding (superheavy nuclei) and in cross-modal phase-locked correlations (³He*/⁴He* Bell tests).

The key claim is that **δ_geom is an independent variable from material properties (bulk conductivity, permittivity, mass density)**, computable from the geometry of the envelope's transition zone (gradient sharpness, topology, symmetry class). This matches the engineering parameter identified independently in IP-001 [Mellinger, internal IP inventory 2026-04-07].
