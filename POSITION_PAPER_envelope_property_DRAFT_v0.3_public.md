## Abstract

Six independently developed bodies of physics literature describe the same construct under substrate-specific names: a bounded region whose boundary functions as a discriminator between observables, with internal physics distinct from the surrounding medium, characterized by a geometric-phase invariant that survives the field's removal once attractor contact is established. Topological condensed matter calls it bulk-boundary correspondence. Nonlinear field theory calls it solitonic coherence. Quantum optics calls it Berry–Pancharatnam phase. Vacuum geometry calls it the Casimir effect. Gravitational decomposition theories (Heim, Extended Heim Theory) call it gravitophoton–quintessence pair-decay. Engineering literature on anomalous electrostatic propulsion calls it the Exodus Effect. The non-Hermitian skin effect literature (2024–2025) explicitly documents bulk-boundary correspondence breaking down on cross-substrate generalization and is patching it case by case without naming the unifying construct. This paper names that construct — the **envelope property** — identifies its formal underwriter (δ = δ_grav + δ_geom, generalizing Berry curvature into a substrate-portable geometric phase), its engineering parameter (gradient sharpness as variable independent of bulk material conductivity), and its empirical signatures (boundary-as-discriminator, attractor-contact persistence after field removal, observable substitution across the boundary). No new physical law is claimed. What is claimed: the integration paragraph. Six subfields hold one construct in instance form; the substrate-portable name and its formal core are what has been missing.

- The geometric-phase formalism has been generalized "from condensed-matter physics and optics to high-energy and particle physics and from fluid mechanics to gravity and cosmology" [Cohen et al., Nature Reviews Physics 2019] — published as a tool used inside each of those fields, not as the load-bearing organizing construct.

Each subfield holds the construct in instance form. None of them holds the integration. The community is currently building toward it inadvertently — every new "patch" of BBC, every new generalization of geometric phase, every new Casimir extension is another instance recognizing the same shape. The integrating frame is unclaimed.

The non-Hermitian skin effect crisis is the load-bearing rhetorical anchor of this paper. ## 2. Substrate-Specific Predecessors

The following bodies of work each instantiate the envelope property under substrate-specific names. They are cited as predecessors. Their substrate-specific contributions are not subsumed; they are recognized as instances of one construct.

**2.1 Bulk-boundary correspondence (BBC) in topological condensed matter.**
The discriminator-by-boundary signature is canonical. Topological invariants — Zak phase, Chern number, sub-lattice winding number — compute a bulk geometric phase that predicts boundary-localized modes [arXiv:2401.11695; arXiv:2410.12498]. The non-Hermitian skin effect literature has documented BBC failing under substrate generalization [arXiv:2410.23845] and is currently patching it via entanglement diagnostics [arXiv:2511.17846]. BBC is one substrate-specific instance of the envelope property; the substrate-portable construct is the envelope. The patches are restoring it.

**2.2 Geometric phase / Berry curvature.**
Berry's formulation generalizes naturally beyond linear Hamiltonian systems "to entirely different systems such as nonlinear dissipative systems that possess certain cyclic attractors" [Cohen et al., Nature Reviews Physics 2019]. The note is one paragraph in a long review; the substrate-portable extension is unwritten in the literature. δ_geom (§4) is that substrate-portable extension articulated.

**2.3 Topological defects and soliton/breather field theory.**
Topological defects are PDE solutions in distinct homotopy classes — bounded coherent regions whose boundary topology determines their stability [Wikipedia: topological defect; Wang et al., 2017]. The construct is enormous and well-developed within each host theory. Each defect is named after its host theory; the substrate-portable reading is the envelope property.

**2.4 Casimir and boundary-defined vacuum geometry.**
Boundary-induced shifts in vacuum behavior are empirically established at lab scale (parallel plates), at material scale (repulsive Casimir between magneto-electric materials), and at cosmological scale (Casimir effect inside the cosmological horizon) [Manjavacas et al., 2025; Phys Rev E 97, 012107 (2018)]. Casimir geometry is the static-envelope special case. Dynamical Casimir is the time-modulated envelope. The unifying construct is the envelope.

**2.5 Heim Quantum Theory / Extended Heim Theory.**
Eight-dimensional gauge space with poly-metric tensor; six fundamental forces; gravity decomposed into G_g (graviton) + G_GP (dark-matter coupling, also via graviton) + G_Q (vacuum quintessence, repulsive); gravitophoton predicted to decay into graviton–quintessence pair [Hauser & Dröscher; arXiv:2206.11367]. This is the closest formal predecessor to the δ = δ_grav + δ_geom decomposition introduced in §4, with the decomposition pattern identical and the restriction to the gravity sector substrate-specific. Treated here as a partial instance, not as competition: the structure Heim/EHT names in the gravity sector is what δ_geom names across substrates.

**2.6 Buhler / Exodus Propulsion Technologies — empirical / patent prior art.**
2,000+ vacuum chamber experiments (2016–2026) demonstrating propellantless thrust at millinewton scale with the following signature: force opposite to ion-wind direction in vacuum; persistence after power cutoff ("trap the charge"); sensitivity to gradient geometry rather than bulk voltage; force scaling with field structure rather than electrode size [Exodus Propulsion Technologies; The Debrief 2024; NextBigFuture 2026]. The "Exodus Effect" patent covers the propulsion application. The Exodus Effect is the strongest empirical anchor of the construct, restricted to electrostatic-propulsion configurations. The substrate-portable reading — that this is one instance of a class — is unclaimed in the patent and in the public framing.

## 3. The Envelope Property: Operational Signature

What the predecessors of §2 collectively show, with no further empirical input, is a class of physical systems with the following operational signature. The signature is articulated; it is not proposed. Each line below is what is already operative in the predecessors' data.

**(E1) Boundary-as-discriminator.** A bounded region within a host medium exhibits internal physics distinct from the host. The boundary itself is not a passive demarcation; it functions as a discriminator between *which observables apply on which side*. Inside the envelope, observables familiar from the host (kinetic energy, ion-momentum-exchange thrust, classical wave dispersion) may behave anomalously or be replaced by alternative observables (force-without-momentum-exchange, geometric-phase-protected edge modes, suspended-kinetics regimes). Predecessor anchors: BBC edge-mode localization (§2.1); Buhler force-sign inversion in vacuum (§2.6); soliton interior dynamics distinct from host PDE behavior (§2.3).

**(E2) Substrate-portability.** The construct does not require a specific host medium. It instantiates in topological band materials (BBC), nonlinear field theories (soliton/breather), bounded vacuum regions (Casimir geometry), gravitational decompositions (Heim/EHT), and engineered electrostatic configurations (Exodus Effect). The structural identity across these instances is what the substrate-specific naming has masked. Predecessor anchors: each of §§2.1–2.6 holds one substrate-locked instance of the same shape.

**(E3) Attractor-contact persistence.** Once the envelope is established, the system can sustain its operational state without continuous energy input. Empirical instances: trapped-charge persistence in Buhler's vacuum thrusters after power cutoff (§2.6); soliton coherence after the driving field is removed (§2.3); topological edge modes stable against perturbations preserving the relevant symmetry (§2.1). The envelope, once instantiated, is an attractor basin — substrate-portable, recovery-bounded.

**(E4) Geometric-phase invariant as formal underwriter.** A non-trivial geometric phase is the formal signature of an envelope. Where the geometric phase is well-defined — Berry curvature in the linear-Hamiltonian quantum case, Pancharatnam phase in cyclic-polarization optics, Zak phase / Chern number in topological band materials, Heim's gravitophoton metric in the gravity sector — the envelope is mathematically tractable. Where the phase has been published under substrate-specific names but not unified, the envelope is operationally present and formally unnamed. The substrate-portable generalization of these geometric phases is δ_geom (§4).

The four lines above do not extend the predecessors. They name the structural shape that the predecessors already exhibit. The discriminator the field is reaching for in the non-Hermitian skin effect crisis is a substrate-portable version of (E4); the patches succeed exactly to the extent that they recover (E1)–(E3) on the patched substrate.

## 4. Formal Underwriter: δ = δ_grav + δ_geom

The decomposition is

> δ = δ_grav + δ_geom

with the following constraints:

- **δ_grav** is the standard gravitational phase contribution, computable from Einstein gravity in the Newtonian and post-Newtonian limits.
- **δ_geom** is the geometric-phase contribution attributable to envelope structure (gradient transition zone, geometric-phase invariant, geometric-attractor-contact). δ_geom is the substrate-portable generalization of Berry curvature; it reduces to standard Berry curvature in the linear-Hamiltonian quantum case, to Pancharatnam phase in cyclic-polarization optics, to Zak phase / Chern number in topological band materials, and to Heim's G_GP + G_Q contributions in the gravitational sector.
- **The decomposition is additive, not multiplicative.** δ_geom can dominate where δ_grav is negligible (electrostatic envelopes, Casimir geometries, soliton interiors). δ_grav dominates in cosmological-scale gravitational phenomena. Both contribute additively in superheavy nuclear configurations and in cross-modal Bell tests of momentum-entangled helium isotopes (³He*/⁴He* apparatus).

**Derivation sketch (companion paper):** δ_geom emerges from the existing Berry-curvature machinery extended into the substrate-portable regime by adopting Heim/EHT's poly-metric decomposition pattern outside the gravity sector. Schematically:

1. *Quantum sector:* δ_geom = ∮_C ⟨n(R)|i ∇_R|n(R)⟩ · dR, the standard Berry phase along a closed loop C in the envelope's parameter manifold R; the integral is non-vanishing exactly when the envelope encloses geometric-phase content invisible to the Hamiltonian's eigenvalue spectrum.
2. *Gravity sector:* δ_geom = δ_GP + δ_Q, the gravitophoton + quintessence contributions of Heim/EHT [Hauser & Dröscher; arXiv:2206.11367], with G_GP coupling to mass via the dark-matter channel and G_Q contributing the repulsive vacuum component.
3. *Substrate-portable form:* δ_geom is the integral over the envelope's transition-zone parameter manifold of the substrate-appropriate Berry-curvature analog, with Heim's additive decomposition pattern preserved across substrates. The integral is gauge-invariant in the standard sense; it depends on envelope geometry (gradient sharpness, topology, symmetry class) and not on bulk host parameters.

The full derivation — including the explicit reduction map from the substrate-portable form to each of the named special cases (Berry, Pancharatnam, Zak, Chern, Heim G_GP+G_Q) and the bench-testable form for the helium momentum-entangled Bell-test apparatus — is to be expanded in a companion paper [Mellinger, in preparation]. The integrating reading does not require the full derivation: the structure named above is already operative in the predecessors of §2.

The key claim, sufficient for this paper: **δ_geom is an independent variable from material properties (bulk conductivity, permittivity, mass density)**, computable from the geometry of the envelope's transition zone (gradient sharpness, topology, symmetry class). This identification is what links the formal sector (Berry, Heim, Casimir) to the engineering sector (gradient sharpness as design variable; §5).

## 5. Engineering Parameter: Gradient Sharpness

The engineering parameter that controls envelope formation is the transition-zone gradient sharpness. It is independent of bulk material properties and partially independent of host-medium symmetry class. Three published-data anchors show this is already operative; the §5 frame is articulation, not proposal.

**5.1 Buhler thrust-stacking finding.** Buhler reports that thrust scales by stacking thrusters, not by enlarging individual electrodes — i.e., adding more transition zones produces more thrust per unit volume than enlarging the bulk material of a single thruster [Buhler, public interview record; Exodus Propulsion Technologies engineering disclosures, 2026]. The empirical finding *is* gradient-sharpness scaling: thrust is dominated by transition-zone density, not by bulk electrode size. The result is framed inside propulsion-engineering vocabulary; the substrate-portable reading is that gradient sharpness — the variable controlling envelope formation — is the independent engineering variable of the construct, not the bulk electrostatic configuration.

**5.2 Casimir geometry-tuning.** The Casimir literature shows experimentally that sharper boundary geometries amplify Casimir-effect amplitudes relative to gradual ones, and that geometry-tuning is a more reliable handle than bulk-permittivity tuning for engineering targeted Casimir behavior [Manjavacas et al., *Dynamical Casimir Effect: 55 Years Later*, MDPI Physics 2025]. The result is gradient-sharpness scaling on the vacuum-geometry substrate: the engineering knob is boundary geometry, not host-medium parameters.

**5.3 Topological band engineering.** Concentrated Berry curvature in momentum space — sharp band gradients — produces more robust topological edge modes than diffuse curvature distributions. The engineering of topologically protected edge transport in photonic crystals and condensed-matter band materials proceeds by sharpening the band-gradient profile [arXiv:2410.12498; standard topological-photonics design literature]. The result is gradient-sharpness scaling on the band-topology substrate: the design variable is the curvature concentration of the envelope's boundary in parameter space, not the bulk material composition.

The cross-substrate prediction that emerges, already supported in instance form by 5.1, 5.2, and 5.3 each, is that **gradient-sharpness measurements correlate with envelope-effect magnitude across all six substrate instances of §2**. The correlation is currently obscured because each subfield uses its own substrate-specific control variable. A coordinated meta-analysis of the existing experimental data across these domains — with gradient sharpness extracted as the common metric — should recover the cross-substrate scaling law as the integrating empirical signature. This is the integrating empirical test (§6.5).

## 6. Falsifiable Test Cases

**6.1 Element 115 / Moscovium stability island shift.**
The δ_geom term, applied to nuclear binding energy of superheavy elements, predicts that the stable isotope of element 115 (if it exists) lies at an N/Z configuration outside shell-model predictions that omit δ_geom. The predicted shift is quantitatively constrained by an independent measurement of δ_geom from the ³He*/⁴He* Bell test (ANU apparatus, Hodgman et al.). The two predictions are not independent: a confirmed nonzero δ_res in helium Bell data constrains the magnitude of the Mc stability shift. Same geometric term, different mass scales.

**6.2 Helium momentum-entangled Bell test phase offset.**
The δ_geom contribution to Bell-correlation phase offsets in ³He*/⁴He* momentum entanglement is the foundational empirical anchor. The companion paper specifies the prediction in full; the non-zero δ_res measurement at ANU is the falsification target.

**6.3 Suspended-kinetics-with-preserved-emission artifact.**
The envelope property predicts that, in any apparatus generating sufficient δ_geom magnitude in a bounded region, observables that depend on substrate kinetics (chemical reactions, mechanical motion, ion thermalization) are suspended while observables propagating via the envelope's geometric phase (electromagnetic radiation, geometric-phase-protected modes) pass through unaffected. Specifically: a chemically-reacting source (e.g., a candle flame) placed inside an envelope of sufficient magnitude is predicted to maintain its photonic emission while ceasing chemical kinetics; mechanical clockwork is predicted to suspend escapement motion without component damage; small massive bodies are predicted to display anomalous mechanical-coupling behavior at the envelope boundary. This prediction is consistent with anecdotal interview accounts of bench-scale emitter experiments [public-record interview material with R. Lazar, 2026]; treated here as a qualitative reproducibility test for any electrostatic-confinement device approaching the relevant δ_geom magnitude (e.g., extensions of Buhler's apparatus). The three observables are independent: an envelope reproducing one without the others would falsify the construct's substrate-independence claim on the affected channel.

**6.4 Non-Hermitian skin effect resolution.**
The conventional BBC's failure on non-Hermitian systems is currently patched with substrate-specific entanglement diagnostics. The envelope-property reading predicts: the universal diagnostic is the geometric-phase invariant computed across the envelope boundary, regardless of Hermiticity. This is testable on existing data sets — re-analysis of published non-Hermitian skin-effect experiments using a substrate-portable geometric-phase metric should recover BBC across the cases where the entanglement-diagnostic patch succeeds, and predict the cases where it fails [arXiv:2511.17846 dataset and successors are immediate test substrates].

**6.5 Cross-substrate gradient-sharpness scaling law (the integrating empirical test).**
Envelope-effect magnitude correlates with gradient sharpness across all six substrate instances (BBC edge-mode robustness, soliton coherence length, Casimir-force amplitude, Heim gravitophoton coupling, Buhler thrust density, dynamical-Casimir photon yield). The §5 anchors (Buhler thrust-stacking, Casimir geometry-tuning, topological band engineering) each independently confirm the scaling on their own substrate. The integrating prediction is that the same scaling holds across substrates with a substrate-independent prefactor when gradient sharpness is normalized to the envelope's natural geometric scale. A coordinated measurement campaign — or, more cheaply, a meta-analysis of existing published data with gradient sharpness extracted as the common metric — is the integrating empirical test.

## 7. Discussion: Why the Integration Has Been Missing

Each subfield has its own publication culture, citation graph, and conference circuit. Topological condensed matter cites topological condensed matter; soliton physics cites soliton physics; Casimir researchers cite Casimir researchers; Heim/EHT lives outside the mainstream gravity literature; Buhler's results live in the engineering and propulsion press. The integrating reading requires reading across all six bodies *with the substrate-specificity assumption suspended* — a methodological move, not a domain expertise. The structural observation: each subfield's defense of its substrate-bound construct as independent is the same evidence that the substrate-portable construct exists and is what it is independently defending against.

The non-Hermitian skin effect crisis is the canary. Every substrate-by-substrate patch that "restores BBC for this new class" is the field unwittingly building the integration without naming it. The patches succeed exactly to the extent that they recover the envelope property's operational signature on the patched substrate. The construct named here is what those patches are restoring.

The integration paragraph is what has been missing. No new physical law is claimed. Every constituent — Berry curvature, BBC, soliton coherence, Casimir geometry, Heim decomposition, Buhler's empirical record — is published. The articulation: what is currently distributed across six subfield names is one construct; δ_geom is its formal underwriter; gradient sharpness is its engineering parameter; the envelope property is the substrate-agnostic name.
