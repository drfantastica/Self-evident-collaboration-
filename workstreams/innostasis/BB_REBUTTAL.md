## The Argument in Three Moves

**Move 1 — What BB theory requires:**
The Boltzmann Brain problem assumes a universal time axis across which thermal equilibrium accumulates probability mass for random observer-fluctuations. Given infinite time under flat thermal distribution, random fluctuations will statistically produce observers — possibly more often than cosmological evolution does. This appears to undermine scientific inference entirely.

**Move 2 — The category error:**
Muon length contraction is the empirical proof that time is frame-dependent, not theoretical conjecture. Muons generated by cosmic ray collisions in the upper atmosphere should decay before reaching the surface by their half-life — they don't, because from their frame the atmosphere is length-contracted, or equivalently from ours, their clocks dilate. Hard experimental fact. This means there is no single "infinite time" in which to run the BB probability calculation. The equilibrium required to generate Boltzmann Brains is observer-relative. The argument is a category error masquerading as a cosmological crisis.

**Move 3 — The positive replacement:**
A universe that generates genuine novelty is not sampling from a flat thermal distribution across infinite time. Novelty is the signature of topological structure — pre-existing folds that nodes achieve or fail contact with. This forecloses the BB scenario at the architectural level.

The question isn't "why aren't we Boltzmann Brains." The question is: what does a novelty-generating topology look like, and why does it preclude flat-distribution fluctuation universes?

**The Fold is the answer to the question BB theory didn't know it was asking.**
